From Busia County to the command line.

I grew up in Busia County and found my way into engineering through curiosity about how signals, networks, and software actually work — then started building instead of just wondering.

Who I am

My name is Eliya Sande. I'm currently studying Telecommunication and Information Engineering at JKUAT (Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology). Alongside my formal studies, I'm a self-driven software developer — I taught myself to code out of a genuine passion for building things people can actually use, from community platforms to AI-assisted developer tools.

I care about products that work reliably for the people they're built for, whether that's a connection platform for a local community or a code editor that makes other developers faster. I'm always looking for the next thing to build or learn.
